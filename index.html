About Tammy

Experienced care for happy, healthy pets.

Tammy is passionate about helping pets feel safe, comfortable, and cared for while their families are busy or away. Whether it’s a quick walk, a midday check-in, or overnight pet sitting, Tammy brings calm, dependable attention to every visit.

Her services are built around trust, consistency, and genuine love for animals. Tammy understands that every pet has unique needs, and she takes the time to provide personalized care that helps them feel at home.
